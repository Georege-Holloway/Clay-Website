Growth Strategy

Your practice, reviewed properly, with a plan you can act on

A Growth Strategy is a full strategic review of how your practice is found, how it's judged, and what's getting in the way of it growing.

Start with a free 30-minute call Send details about your practice

It starts with a single objective

Before anything gets analysed, we agree what the Strategy is for. Not “improve my marketing.” One measurable objective over a defined period, in your words, agreed out loud.

That objective then becomes the lens for the whole document. Every recommendation has to earn its place by moving you towards it. Anything that doesn't gets cut, or named explicitly as a later phase. It's the difference between a strategy and a checklist.

An example objective

Three new enquiries a month from people looking for trauma therapy locally, within six months.

Not “more clients” or “better marketing”. Something you'll know you've hit.

What gets looked at

Your website

structure, copy, how someone actually makes contact, and whether it holds up on a phone

Search visibility

how you appear in Google, and increasingly what AI assistants say when someone asks them to recommend a therapist

Directory listings

BACP, UKCP, Counselling Directory, Psychology Today and modality-specific bodies, checked for consistency and for services you offer but haven't listed

Google Business Profile and reviews

whether they exist, whether they're claimed, and whether they're visible anywhere that matters

Booking and payments

how a client actually books, how many steps it takes, and how you get paid

Your competitive set

who a client in your area is genuinely choosing between, what they offer and what they charge

What Clay clients say

How a Growth Strategy works

  1. 1

    Free 30-minute call

    We talk through your practice and what you want from it. You get an honest answer on whether a Growth Strategy is the right fit, or whether something else would serve you better.

  2. 2

    Discovery call

    A 45 to 60 minute conversation about how your practice works today. We finish by agreeing one measurable goal: the thing that will tell us the work has succeeded.

  3. 3

    Proposal and payment

    Your goal, what the report will cover and what it won't, confirmed in writing. The fee is £1,200 and can be split into two instalments. Work starts once the first is paid.

  4. 4

    Research and analysis

    I review your website, search performance, Google Business Profile, directory listings, reviews and how clients book with you. I'll ask for view-only access to Google Search Console and Google Analytics if you have them. Nothing about your clients is ever needed.

  5. 5

    Report and walkthrough

    A written report with evidenced findings and recommendations phased now, next and later, each one tested against your goal. I walk you through it on a call, so you can ask questions rather than reading it alone.

Typically 10 working days from payment to walkthrough, once access to your accounts is in place.

After the report

The report is yours to act on however you like. Some clients do the work themselves, some pass it to their own designer or developer, and some ask me to do it: fixing directory listings, setting up a Google Business Profile, or rewriting key pages. Clay's hands-on support is scoped as a fixed project at a fixed price, agreed separately. If you'd rather keep going with guidance, Growth Sessions are there too. There's no obligation and no subscription.

Who it's for, who it isn't

Who it's for

Established practitioners and therapy organisations: solo practices that have plateaued, practices fully booked on word of mouth with no online presence to fall back on, practitioners losing hours to admin, and anyone niching down, adding a modality or launching group or training work.

Who it isn't for

If you're still in training, or newly qualified and not yet seeing clients, a Growth Strategy won't tell you much you couldn't work out yourself. Come back when there's a practice to look at. The resources are free in the meantime.

If you want a website that looks better without changing anything underneath it, I'm probably not the right person.

Common questions

How much does a Growth Strategy cost?

£1,200, as a flat fee. Solo practice or larger organisation, the price doesn't change. What varies by engagement is scope, and that's agreed and written down at the discovery call.

What's the difference between a Growth Session and a Growth Strategy?

A Growth Session is a one-hour call to work through whatever is on your mind, with a written summary afterwards, for £120. A Growth Strategy is a full review of your practice built around one agreed goal, delivered as a written report with phased recommendations and a walkthrough call, for £1,200. Sessions suit ongoing questions; a Strategy suits needing to know what's actually happening and what to do first.

Can Clay carry out the recommendations after the report?

Yes, if you want it to. After a Growth Strategy, Clay can take on any of the recommendations as a fixed project at a fixed price, scoped from the report itself. Many clients do some of the work themselves and hand over the parts they'd rather not do.

Will it tell me whether Counselling Directory or Psychology Today is worth paying for?

Yes, for your practice specifically. The usual answer is that the platform is fine and the profile isn't. A thin bio with generic copy competes badly against a practitioner who has invested in theirs. Worth checking which it is before cancelling anything.

Can you do this for a group practice or training organisation?

Yes. The scope is larger and there are usually more stakeholders, so the discovery stage includes working out who needs to agree the objective.
