Launch your practice

You're newly qualified, or about to be, and trained to hold space for people, not to build websites, manage an increasingly busy calendar, file self-assessments, or figure out how to market yourself.

Most therapists going private end up piecing together a website builder, a scheduling tool, a payment processor, a directory listing, an accountant, and a lot of late evenings. It works... eventually. But it costs time you'd rather spend with clients, and money on tools that don't talk to each other.

Clay brings it into one place. One setup. One monthly fee. Built and run personally by George Holloway — someone who understands both the clinical world and the business one.

What’s Included

At the setup stage

  • A professional website built and hosted on your own domain
  • Home, About and Contact pages written and designed around your practice, using your own words if you'd like to write them, or drafted for you if you'd rather we take the lead
  • Google Business Profile set up so you show up in local search
  • Search Engine Optimisation (SEO) so your site can be found by the right clients

Every month after that

  • Hosting and maintenance so there's nothing for you to manage
  • Website content updates whenever your fees, availability, or specialisms change
  • Ongoing visibility support to keep your Google Business Profile and SEO working as your practice grows
  • Email support whenever you need it
  • No hidden platform costs
  • No fixed contracts — cancel anytime
