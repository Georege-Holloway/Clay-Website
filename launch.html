Launch your practice

You're newly qualified, or about to be, and trained to hold space for people, not to build websites, manage an increasingly busy calendar, file self-assessments, or figure out how to market yourself.

Most therapists going private end up piecing together a website builder, a scheduling tool, a payment processor, a directory listing, an accountant, and a lot of late evenings. It works... eventually. But it costs time you'd rather spend with clients, and money on tools that don't talk to each other.

Clay brings it into one place. One setup. One monthly fee. Built and run personally by George Holloway — someone who understands both the clinical world and the business one.
