Common questions

Do I own my website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the site is yours. If you leave, you keep it.

What happens if I cancel?

No notice period and no exit fee. Hosting and updates stop at the end of the month you cancel in.

Do I need a website if I'm already on a directory?

Directories are where most private clients start looking, and they are worth paying for. But every profile on Counselling Directory looks broadly the same, and a client comparing three therapists has very little to go on.

A website is where you say the specific thing a directory template will not let you say. It is also what people check after they have found you, to confirm you are real.

Can I write my own copy?

Yes. Plenty of therapists would rather write about their own practice, and that often produces better results. Clay edits and shapes it. If you would rather not, it gets drafted for you and you approve it.
