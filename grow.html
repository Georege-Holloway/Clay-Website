Grow your offering

Grow is a business health check for established therapy practices and organisations: a structured audit of what's working, what isn't, and where growth is actually being held back. Delivered personally by George Holloway, and scoped to fit your practice, with a clear price agreed before anything starts.

Why growth stalls

Almost nothing stalls because the work itself isn't good enough. It stalls because the things around the work stopped keeping up.

A website built years ago that no longer reflects how you work — or who you serve. Directory listings half-finished or pointing the wrong way. A booking or referral process with just enough friction that enquiries quietly drop off. Admin and operations spread across enough different tools that it's eating time without anyone deciding it should.

None of it dramatic. Each one just a small, invisible tax on growth.

The hard part isn't fixing these things. It's seeing them. When you're inside a practice or an organisation every day, the friction becomes background noise — normal, unnoticed, and very hard to prioritise. What's missing usually isn't effort. It's a clear, outside look at where ground is actually being lost.

That's what a Health Check is for.

A structured look across the whole operation — not just the obvious bits — to find where growth is actually being held back.

What we look at

1

How you're found

Your website, your search visibility, your directory listings, your Google presence. The whole path a potential client travels before they ever reach your booking page, and the points along it where they're quietly dropping off.

2

How you convert

What happens once someone does find you. Whether your online presence sounds like you, whether it speaks to the people you want to reach, and whether enquiring or booking is effortless or just friction-y enough to lose people.

3

How you run

The day-to-day machinery: booking, payments, communications, admin. Where time is going, what's spread across too many tools, and what could quietly run itself instead of running you.

4

Where you're heading

Your positioning and your direction. Whether your public-facing presence still reflects who you've become, and whether there's a sharper, more deliberate direction worth moving toward.

It all comes back to you as one prioritised report — not a list of problems, but a clear order of what to do first.

A Practice Health Check isn't a conversation that evaporates, it's something concrete you can act on.

What you walk away with

1

A written report

A clear, readable assessment across all four areas. No jargon, no padding — just an honest picture of where things stand and what's holding growth back.

2

A prioritised action plan

The part that actually matters: everything we've found, put in order. What to do first, what can wait, and what to leave well alone. Quick wins flagged separately from the bigger projects.

3

A walkthrough call

An hour together to talk through the report properly — so it's not a document you read once and file away. A chance to ask questions, push back, and decide what you want to take forward.

The whole thing is straightforward: a short intro call, a look under the bonnet of your practice, then your report and walkthrough — usually within a couple of weeks of starting.

And if you decide you'd like help putting the plan into action, that's a conversation we can have, but there's no obligation, and no pressure to.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this just a sales pitch for ongoing services?

No. A Health Check is a defined piece of work with a defined outcome, and it's genuinely useful on its own. If you'd like help putting the plan into action afterwards, that's a separate conversation — but there's no obligation, and the report is yours either way.

My practice is doing fine — is a Health Check still worth it?

Often that's exactly when it's most useful. When nothing is obviously broken, the small friction points are hardest to see, because they've become background noise. A Health Check gives you an outside look at where ground is quietly being lost.

Will I be told everything I'm doing wrong?

No. The report covers what's working well as much as what isn't, and it flags the things that genuinely aren't worth changing. The aim is a clear picture and a sensible order of priorities, not a list of criticisms.

How long does it take?

Usually a couple of weeks from the intro call to your report and walkthrough, depending on the size of the practice or organisation.

What if I don't agree with the recommendations?

That's what the walkthrough call is for. It's an hour to talk the report through properly, ask questions, and push back. You know your practice better than anyone; the report is there to inform your decisions, not make them for you.

Do I have to commit to anything afterwards?

Not at all. One price, agreed upfront, with nothing attached and nothing to cancel. What you do with the plan is entirely up to you.

We’re ready when you are

Start with a short, no-pressure call. A chance to talk through where your practice is now and what a Health Check would look at. If it sounds useful, we'll take it from there. If not, no harm done.
