Who we are

Clay Consulting is a UK marketing and business strategy consultancy helping therapists, counsellors and therapy organisations grow their private practice.

Clay is run by George Holloway, who's currently training as a psychotherapist at CCPE, alongside thirteen years in commercial marketing and strategy, most recently as a Senior Growth Manager at Amazon. Most people come to Clay for that combination.

Why this matters

4 in 10UK businesses don't make it past their fifth year. The most common reason isn't lack of skill. It's admin, cash flow, and decisions made without the right information.

For therapists, the risk is sharper. Training prepares you to hold space for clients, not to run a business. Practitioners end up doing both, without HR, finance or marketing teams to lean on.

Sources: ONS Business Demography (2024); BACP Private Practice Toolkit
