Who we are

Clay Consulting is a marketing and business strategy consultancy for therapists, counsellors, and therapy organisations in the UK.

We run two services. Launch, for newly qualifying practitioners who need a website, SEO, and Google Business Profile set up from day one, from £99 setup + £25/month. And Grow, a business health check and growth strategy for established practices and organisations.

Clay was founded by George Holloway, who spent over a decade in commercial marketing — most recently as a Senior Growth Manager at Amazon — alongside training as a psychotherapist at CCPE.

Why this matters

Around 4 in 10 UK businesses don't make it past their fifth year. The most common reason isn't lack of skill, it's admin, cash flow problems, and decisions made without the right information.

For counsellors, therapists, and organisations, the risk is sharper. Training prepares you to hold space for clients, not to run a business. New practitioners end up doing both, without the expertise of HR, finance, or marketing teams to lean on.

We've seen it up close — not from the outside looking in, but from inside a psychotherapy training programme, watching this unfold firsthand.

Clay exists so the business side never gets in the way of the work you trained for.

Sources: ONS Business Demography (2024); BACP Private Practice Toolkit
