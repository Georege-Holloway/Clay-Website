Who we are

Commercial strategy, built for therapists

Clay Consulting is a UK marketing and business strategy consultancy helping therapists, counsellors and therapy organisations grow their private practice.

Clay is run by George Holloway, who's currently training as a psychotherapist at CCPE, alongside thirteen years in commercial marketing and strategy, most recently as a Senior Growth Manager at Amazon. Most people come to Clay for that combination.

What we cover

1

Foundations

We look at whether your pricing, positioning and the way you describe your work match the clients you want. Then we help you put it right: setting fees you can sustain, and rewriting your website and directory profiles so they explain what makes your work different.

2

Visibility

We look at whether the right people can find you, on Google, in directories and in AI assistants like ChatGPT. Then we do the work: claiming and completing your Google Business Profile, setting up or correcting your Psychology Today and Counselling Directory listings, strengthening your local search, and shaping your content so AI tools can recommend you.

3

Enquiries

We look at what happens between someone finding you and booking a first session. Then we fix what's losing people: missing prices, broken booking links, pages that compete with each other, and booking or payment that takes too many steps.

Every piece of work starts from one agreed goal, so nothing gets done just because it's on a checklist.

What Clay clients say

Frequently asked questions

Who does Clay work with?

Clay works with therapists, counsellors, psychotherapists and therapy organisations across the UK, from trainees and newly qualified practitioners to established practices, CICs and training organisations. All work is done online.

What's the difference between a Growth Session and a Growth Strategy?

A Growth Session is a one-hour call to work through whatever is on your mind, with a written summary afterwards, for £120. A Growth Strategy is a full review of your practice built around one agreed goal, delivered as a written report with phased recommendations and a walkthrough call, for £1,200. Sessions suit ongoing questions; a Strategy suits needing to know what's actually happening and what to do first.

Can Clay do the work, or just advise?

Both. Clay can carry out the recommendations from your Growth Strategy, or take on a specific job you bring, such as setting up your Google Business Profile or rewriting your directory listings. Hands-on work is scoped as a fixed project at a fixed price, agreed before it starts.

Can you help my practice get recommended by ChatGPT and other AI assistants?

Yes. More people now ask AI assistants to recommend a therapist, and those tools favour practices whose websites and listings answer specific questions clearly. Clay's own clients mostly find it through ChatGPT, and the same approach is applied to your practice.

Can you set up my Google Business Profile or Psychology Today listing?

Yes. Clay sets up, claims and corrects Google Business Profiles and directory listings, including Psychology Today, Counselling Directory and BACP. The aim is that all of them describe the same practice, in the same place, with the same specialism.

Do you build websites?

Only where a Growth Strategy shows that a new site is what's needed. Most of the time, improving the website you already have does more, and costs less.

Do you use AI to write the reports?

George uses AI tools to speed up research and drafting, the same way he uses any other tool. What isn't outsourced is the thinking: every finding comes from your own practice's data, and every recommendation is George's judgement, reviewed and signed off by him before it reaches you. He spent eight years at Amazon writing strategy documents, and applies the same standard to every report.

Will I need to share anything about my clients?

No. Clay never needs clinical or client information. The work uses your website, directory listings and, with your permission, view-only access to tools like Google Search Console and Google Analytics.

Can you guarantee more clients?

No, and anyone who does is guessing. What Clay can do is find what's holding your practice back, agree one measurable goal with you, and focus every recommendation on reaching it.

Not sure where to start?

Most people aren't, until they've talked it through. A short call costs nothing and tells you what's actually worth doing.
