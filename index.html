Why this matters

Around 4 in 10 UK businesses don't make it past their fifth year. The most common reason isn't lack of skill. It's admin, cash flow, and decisions made without the right information.

For therapists, the risk is sharper. Training prepares you to hold space for clients, not to run a business. Practitioners end up doing both, without HR, finance or marketing teams to lean on.

I've seen it from inside a psychotherapy training programme, not from the outside looking in.

Sources: ONS Business Demography (2024); BACP Private Practice Toolkit
