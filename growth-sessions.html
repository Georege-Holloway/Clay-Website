Growth Sessions

A regular hour to think about the business side

£120 per session · one hour · video call

Running a practice throws up a steady stream of decisions that have nothing to do with the clinical work. Whether to raise your fees. Whether the directory subscription is worth keeping. What to do about the enquiries that don't convert. Whether now is the right time to add a specialism, or a group, or a second room.

Most practitioners work through those alone, or in the gaps between clients. Growth Sessions are an hour set aside to do it properly, with someone who understands both halves of what you're building.

How it works

You bring what's actually on your mind: a decision you're stuck on, something that isn't working, a plan you want tested before you commit to it. We talk it through, and I tell you what I'd do and why, including the things I'd leave alone.

Afterwards you get a short written summary: what we covered, what we agreed, and the specific things worth working on before we next speak.

Sessions or a Strategy?

Sessions

Growth Sessions are a thinking partner. They work from what you bring, and they're most useful when you know roughly what the questions are and want someone to help you answer them well.

Or a Strategy?

A Growth Strategy is a piece of research. I look at your actual website, your search visibility, your directory listings and your competitors, and write up what I find with prioritised recommendations. It's for when you need evidence rather than direction.

Plenty of clients only ever do one. Others use Sessions to work out what they need, then move to a Strategy, or ask Clay to do a specific piece of work once it's clear. Nothing leads automatically to anything else. See how all the options compare on the homepage.

What Clay clients say

Common questions

Do I have to commit to anything?

No. Growth Sessions can be booked one at a time or monthly, and stopped whenever you want. There's no block booking, contract or subscription.

Is it cheaper to book monthly?

No. Every Growth Session is £120, whichever cadence you choose.

Do I need a free call first?

No. You can book a Growth Session directly. If you'd rather understand the options before spending anything, a free 30-minute call is available.

How do payment and cancellation work?

Sessions are paid by card when you book, through Stripe. Cancel or reschedule at least 48 hours before your session for a full refund or one free reschedule. Sessions cancelled with less than 48 hours' notice aren't refundable.

Is this business supervision?

No. Supervision is a clinical relationship with its own professional standards. Growth Sessions are commercial guidance for the business side of a practice, and aren't a substitute for clinical supervision.

What do I get in writing?

After each session, George sends a short summary of what was discussed, what was agreed, and the specific things to work on before the next one.

Can Clay do the work we talk about?

Yes. If a session surfaces a specific job, such as setting up a Google Business Profile or rewriting a directory listing, Clay can take it on as a fixed project at a fixed price, agreed separately.

Can an organisation use this?

Yes. Group practices, CICs and training organisations can book Growth Sessions, and more than one person can join the call.

What if I run out of things to bring?

Then stop, or leave a longer gap between sessions. George would rather you paused and came back when something comes up than kept a standing slot out of habit.
