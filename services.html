Frequently asked questions

How do I get more counselling clients?

Usually it isn't one thing. It's a combination of how easily a prospective client can find you, whether your website and directory listings give them enough to decide to contact you, and whether your pricing and positioning match the clients you actually want. A Growth Session or Growth Blueprint identifies which of these is holding you back, rather than guessing.

How much should I charge for therapy sessions?

This depends on your location, modality, experience and the clients you want to attract, and there is no single correct answer. What matters more is whether your current fee matches your positioning. A therapist charging low fees while marketing specialist trauma work, for example, is usually undercharging. This is one of the things covered in a Growth Session or Growth Blueprint.

Are you a therapist yourself?

I am training in psychotherapy at CCPE alongside thirteen years in commercial marketing and strategy, including at Amazon. Most people come to me because of that combination rather than for either half on its own.
