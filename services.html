How I work with therapists and counsellors

Most people come to me with one of a few problems. Not enough clients. A website that isn't bringing anyone in. A caseload that has stopped growing. Money going out on directories and advertising with no way of telling whether any of it works.

I work with qualified therapists, counsellors and therapy organisations on the business side of practice. I trained in psychotherapy at CCPE and spent thirteen years in commercial marketing and strategy before that, most recently at Amazon. That combination is the reason people come to me rather than to a general business consultant or a marketing agency.

There are three ways to work with me. They run in sequence, and you can stop at any point.

A free 15-minute call

A short conversation so I can understand your practice and what you are trying to change.

I will ask what you do, how full your caseload is, where your clients currently come from, and what made you get in touch now. At the end I will tell you honestly whether I can help, and which of the options below makes sense.

This is not a sales call and I do not use it to give advice. Fifteen minutes is enough to work out whether there is something here worth paying for, and if there isn't, I will say so.

Cost: free. Duration: 15 minutes.

A strategy session

One hour, on a video call. You leave with a single clear objective and the two or three things that will move you towards it.

Most practitioners I speak to know something needs to change but have not defined what success would look like. “More clients” is not an objective. “Eight privately funded clients a week by March, at £90 a session” is, and it changes every decision that follows.

In the hour we will cover where the practice is now, what you want it to look like, and what is getting in the way. We will agree the objective out loud before the call ends. I will then tell you what I would do about it, and just as importantly what I would stop doing, because most practices are spending time and money on things that were never going to work for them.

Afterwards you get a short written summary of the objective and the actions we agreed.

Everything in the session comes from our conversation and my experience of the sector. It is direction rather than evidence. If you need to know what is actually happening with your website, your search visibility or your competitors, that is the Health Check.

Cost: £120. Duration: 1 hour.

A Practice Health Check

A full strategic review of your practice's commercial and digital setup, delivered as a written report.

It starts with a longer discovery call and the same anchoring objective. Then I look at the things a prospective client encounters when they are deciding whether to contact you, and at the routes by which they find you in the first place:

  • Your website: structure, copy, how someone actually makes contact, and whether it stands up on a phone
  • Search visibility, including how you appear in Google and in AI assistants like ChatGPT
  • Your directory listings across BACP, UKCP, Counselling Directory, Psychology Today and modality-specific bodies
  • Your Google Business Profile and reviews
  • How a client books and how you get paid
  • Your competitive set: who else a client in your area is choosing between, what they offer and what they charge

You receive a report with prioritised recommendations, phased into what to do now, next and later. Every finding has evidence behind it, and I walk you through it on a call rather than emailing it over.

The report tells you what to do. It does not include doing it. Where the work is something I can take on, such as rebuilding a website, we agree that separately afterwards. There is no obligation either way, and no subscription attached to any of it.

Cost: scoped per engagement and agreed before anything starts. A solo practice and a training organisation are not the same size of problem and the price reflects that. If you have already had a strategy session, the £120 comes off in full, as long as you go ahead within three months.

What happens afterwards

Some clients take the report and act on it themselves. Some want me to stay involved, either to do the work or to keep an eye on how it is going. Both are fine, and neither is decided upfront.

Who this is not for

If you are still in training, or newly qualified and not yet seeing clients, a Health Check will not tell you much you cannot work out yourself. Come back when there is a practice to look at, and in the meantime read the resources, which are free.

If you are looking for someone to make your website look better without changing anything else, I am probably not the right person.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get more counselling clients?

Usually it isn't one thing. It's a combination of how easily a prospective client can find you, whether your website and directory listings give them enough to decide to contact you, and whether your pricing and positioning match the clients you actually want. A strategy session or Health Check identifies which of these is holding you back, rather than guessing.

Why isn't my therapy website working?

Most therapy websites fail for one of three reasons: nobody can find them, the person who finds them can't work out what to do next, or the site doesn't say anything that distinguishes the practitioner from the dozens of others in the same directory. A Health Check identifies which of these applies to your site specifically, with evidence.

How much should I charge for therapy sessions?

This depends on your location, modality, experience and the clients you want to attract, and there is no single correct answer. What matters more is whether your current fee matches your positioning. A therapist charging low fees while marketing specialist trauma work, for example, is usually undercharging. This is one of the things covered in a strategy session or Health Check.

Is it worth paying for Counselling Directory or Psychology Today?

For most practitioners, yes, but only if the profile is actually working. A directory listing with a thin bio, no recent updates and generic copy competes badly against practitioners who have invested in theirs. Before cancelling a directory subscription, it's worth checking whether the problem is the platform or the profile.

What's the difference between a strategy session and a Health Check?

A strategy session is one hour and gives you direction: a clear objective and a handful of actions, based on what you tell me. A Health Check is a full audit with evidence: your website, search visibility, directory listings, Google Business Profile and competitors, reviewed in detail and delivered as a written report. If you already know roughly what's wrong, the session is usually enough. If you need to know what's actually happening, the Health Check is the right starting point.

Do you build websites?

Where a Health Check identifies that a new or rebuilt website is the right next step, yes, I can take that on. It isn't sold as a standalone product and there's no obligation to use me for it. Some clients prefer to brief their own developer using the report's findings.

Are you a therapist yourself?

Yes. I trained in psychotherapy at CCPE alongside thirteen years in commercial marketing and strategy, including at Amazon. Most people come to me because of that combination rather than for either half on its own.

Is there a contract or ongoing commitment?

No. Every engagement is priced and agreed upfront, with no subscription attached. Some clients choose to continue with ongoing advisory work afterwards, but nothing here requires it.
