Your free practice setup guide: everything a newly qualifying UK therapist needs

30 June 2026 · George Holloway

Setting up a private practice for the first time involves more moving parts than most training courses cover. Registration, insurance, GDPR compliance, fees, directories — it all needs to happen in roughly the right order, before you see your first client.

We put this guide together to make that process straightforward. It covers everything a newly qualifying therapist in the UK needs to have in place, with projected costs, the legal requirements, and the practical steps that often get missed.
