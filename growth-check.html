What is the Growth Check?

The Growth Check is a free online assessment from Clay Consulting for therapists, counsellors, psychotherapists and other wellbeing practitioners in private practice in the UK. It asks about 25 questions about how your practice finds clients, turns enquiries into bookings and runs day to day. It scores six areas out of ten and identifies the one area with most room to grow. You see your results as soon as you finish, and receive a full personalised report by email.

Is it for you?

The Growth Check is for practitioners at any stage, from ten years in practice to still training. It's most useful if any of these sound familiar:

  • Enquiries come in waves: busy one month, quiet the next
  • People get in touch but don't book a first session
  • Your diary has gaps you can't seem to fill
  • You're busy, but the income doesn't reflect the work
  • You're not sure which of the things you've tried are actually working
  • You're about to start private practice and want to know what to get in place first

If you haven't started seeing private clients yet, you'll get a shorter set of questions about setting up, so you aren't marked down for not having clients yet.

What you get

As soon as you finish, you'll see your overall score, a score for each area, your biggest opportunity and your strongest area. Enter your email to receive your full personalised report, which you can also download straight away. It includes:

  • Your overall score, and a score out of ten for each area
  • Your biggest opportunity: the area most likely to be limiting your practice, and why
  • Your strongest area: what's already working in your favour
  • Any warning signs in your answers worth looking at soon
  • A page for each area, explaining what your answers suggest and the signs to look for
  • Where to focus, and what to avoid, in each area

What the Growth Check can and can't tell you

The Growth Check shows you where to focus. It doesn't tell you exactly how to fix things, because that depends on things a questionnaire can't see: your website, your directory listings, the other therapists near you, and what you want from your practice.

The report is automated, but it isn't written by AI. The questions, scoring and report were designed by George Holloway, the founder of Clay. Your results come from fixed rules and pre-written text, so two practitioners who give the same answers get the same report.

If you want someone to look at the full picture, that's what Clay's paid work is for.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Growth Check free?

Yes. You don't need to give payment details, and there's no obligation to buy anything afterwards.

How long does it take?

There are about 25 questions, or about 15 if you haven't started seeing private clients yet. Most people finish in around five minutes.

Why isn't my therapy practice growing?

Most private practices that stall do so for one of a small number of reasons: not enough enquiries are reaching them, enquiries aren't becoming clients, people can't find them online, or it isn't clear who the practice is for. Less often, the practice is full but the fees or the admin are the problem. From the inside, these can look alike, because a quiet diary can come from any of them. The Growth Check scores each one, so you can see which is most likely in your case.

Is my report written by AI?

No. Your report is built from fixed rules and text written in advance, so it's consistent and based only on your answers.

Can I take it before I've started private practice?

Yes. If you're training, or haven't seen private clients yet, you'll get a shorter version focused on what to have in place before you start.

How is it different from a Growth Strategy?

The Growth Check is a free, automated self-assessment based on your own answers. A Growth Strategy is a paid, in-depth review by George of your actual practice: your website, directory listings, search visibility and local competition, with a written plan built around one clear objective. The Growth Check tells you where to look. A Growth Strategy does the looking.

How is it scored?

Each answer scores between 0 and 10, and each area's score is the average of its answers. “Don't know” answers score low on purpose, because not knowing where your enquiries come from, for example, is useful to find out.

What happens to my answers?

Your answers and contact details are stored securely and used to create your report. We'll only send you marketing emails if you tick the box to say you'd like them, and you can ask us to delete your data at any time. See our privacy notice.

Will you contact me afterwards?

You'll receive your report by email. Beyond that, we'll only be in touch if you've asked us to.

Can I take it again?

Yes. Many practitioners find it useful to retake every few months and see what's changed.
