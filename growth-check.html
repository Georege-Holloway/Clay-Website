Find out what's actually holding your practice back

Most practitioners know something isn't working. Far fewer can say which thing. “I need more clients” can mean nobody can find you, or that plenty of people find you and don't get in touch, or that you're spending hours on admin that should be spent on clients. Those are three different problems with three different answers.

The Growth Check is a short set of questions that tells you which one you're dealing with.

How it works

Twelve questions about your practice, your website, where your clients come from and how much time your admin takes. Nothing technical, nothing you need to look up. It takes about three minutes.

At the end you get a result telling you which area is most likely holding your growth back, and why that pattern tends to show up.

What it tells you, and what it doesn't

The Check gives you the category. It's genuinely useful for knowing where to look, and it costs nothing.

What it can't do is look at your actual website, your search visibility or your competitors. It's working from your answers, not from evidence. So it will tell you that your problem is likely visibility; it won't tell you that your site isn't indexed, or that three competitors in your area rank above you for the term your clients actually search. That's what the Growth Blueprint does.

What happens after

Nothing automatic. No sequence of emails, no call unless you ask for one.

If the result is useful on its own, take it and act on it. If you want to talk it through, book a free 15-minute call — bring your result and we'll start from there rather than from scratch. If you already know you want ongoing support or a full review, the Growth Sessions and Growth Blueprint are the two ways to work with me.

Common questions

Is the Growth Check really free?

Yes. No card details, no trial, nothing to cancel.

How accurate can twelve questions be?

Accurate about the category, not about the specifics. The questions are built around the patterns that come up repeatedly across therapy practices, so it's reliable at telling you whether you're looking at a visibility, conversion or operations problem. It isn't a substitute for someone looking at your actual setup.

I'm still in training — is this useful for me?

Less so. The Check assumes there's a practice to assess. If you're pre-qualification, the resources are free and more relevant.

Who sees my answers?

Only me. They aren't shared or sold, and the privacy policy sets out what's stored and for how long.
