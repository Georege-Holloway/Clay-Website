Free. No payment details. Results on screen straight away, full report by email.

The Growth Check is a free online assessment from Clay Consulting for therapists, counsellors and psychotherapists in private practice in the UK. It scores six areas of your practice out of ten and identifies the one with most room to grow.

Is it for you?

It's most useful if any of these sound familiar:

  • Enquiries come in waves: busy one month, quiet the next
  • People get in touch but don't book a first session
  • You're busy, but the income doesn't reflect the work
  • You're about to start private practice and want to know what to get in place first

The six areas it looks at

  • Client Flow: how many new enquiries reach you, and where from
  • Conversion: how many suitable enquiries become clients
  • Visibility: whether people can find you on Google, directories and AI assistants like ChatGPT
  • Positioning: how clearly people can tell who your practice is for
  • Business Performance: whether your income reflects the time you put in
  • Practice Systems: booking, payments, records and admin

What you get

A personalised report, which you can download straight away and also receive by email, covering:

  • Your overall score and a score for each area
  • Your biggest opportunity, and why
  • Your strongest area
  • Any warning signs worth looking at soon
  • Where to focus, and what to avoid, in each area

Frequently asked questions

Why isn't my therapy practice growing?

Most practices that stall do so for one of a few reasons: not enough enquiries, enquiries not becoming clients, people not finding you online, or it not being clear who the practice is for. From the inside these look alike, because a quiet diary can come from any of them. The Growth Check scores each one, so you can see which is most likely in your case.

Is my report written by AI?

No. It's built from fixed rules and text written in advance, so it's based only on your answers, and two people who give the same answers get the same report.

Can I take it before I've started private practice?

Yes. If you're training, or haven't seen private clients yet, you'll get a shorter version about what to have in place before you start.

How is it different from a Growth Strategy?

The Growth Check is a free, automated self-assessment based on your answers. A Growth Strategy is a paid, in-depth review by George of your actual practice: your website, listings, search visibility and local competition. The Growth Check tells you where to look. A Growth Strategy does the looking.

What happens to my answers?

They're stored securely and used to create your report. We'll only send you anything else if you tick the box to say you'd like it, and you can ask us to delete your data at any time. See our privacy notice.
